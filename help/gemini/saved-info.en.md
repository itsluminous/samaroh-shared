# Gemini "Saved info" - English

Paste each block below as ONE entry in Gemini → Settings → Saved info (gemini.google.com/saved-info,
or in the Gemini app: profile picture → Saved info). Keep entries short; Gemini reads them on every
chat, including when it is summoned over another app on the phone.

I use the Samaroh app (Android app and web app) to run my hall/venue business: bookings, payments, expenses, inventory, notes and files. When I ask how to do something in Samaroh, answer with short numbered steps, naming the exact buttons. The full guide is at https://itsluminous.github.io/samaroh-shared/help/en/index.html - fetch the page for the screen I am on if you can.

---

Samaroh basics: the bottom bar has Booking, Expenses, Inventory, Notes and Files. The Menu (Settings, Reports, Members, About) opens from the three-dots button at the top right. On the phone many buttons are icons - pressing and holding an icon shows its name.

---

Samaroh payments: open the booking on the calendar → booking card → Record payment (₹ icon) → Amount, Date, Method (Cash / UPI / Bank transfer / Cheque / Other) → Save. The invoice is on the same card: Invoice → Share PDF or Share as text. Guide: https://itsluminous.github.io/samaroh-shared/help/en/booking_card.html

---

Samaroh inventory: stock count is changed with Record transaction (plus button on the Inventory tab) → pick the Item, choose Add or Remove, Quantity, Unit price → Save. New items are added in the Item list (list icon at top right of Inventory, or the Master list button on the web) → plus / Add item → Name, Unit, Choose photo → Save. Guide: https://itsluminous.github.io/samaroh-shared/help/en/inventory_stock.html

---

Samaroh files: Files tab → open the folder → Upload (round button at bottom right on the phone) → pick the files. From WhatsApp: share the file → Save to Samaroh → Save to Files → pick folder → Upload here. Guide: https://itsluminous.github.io/samaroh-shared/help/en/files.html
